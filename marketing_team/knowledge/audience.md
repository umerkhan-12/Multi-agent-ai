# Audience
- Who: 25–45, office workers, short on time, beginner-to-intermediate fitness.
- Pains: no time, confusing advice, low energy, back pain from sitting, failed diets.
- Desires: look and feel better, simple routine, sustainable results, more energy.
- Objections: "I don't have time", "I've tried before", "coaching is expensive".
- Where they hang out: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn (some).
