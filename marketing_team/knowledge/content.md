# Content Pillars & Formats
1. Myth-busting (e.g. "Zone 2 myths") — Reels/TikTok, 30–45s.
2. Quick wins (e.g. "Meal prep in 20 min", "5 stretches after a run") — Carousels.
3. Day-in-the-life / behind the scenes (e.g. "Filming my morning") — TikTok.
4. Simple habits (e.g. "Walk 30 min a day") — Shorts.
- Hook formula: pattern interrupt in the first 2 seconds + a specific promise.
- Every script: Hook → 3 points → CTA from offer.md.
- Mix per week: 3 myth-busting, 2 quick wins, 1 day-in-life, 1 habit.
