# Brand Voice
<!-- Replace everything below with YOUR brand. Agents read this before writing anything. -->
- Brand: Jev Fitness — evidence-based fitness coaching for busy professionals.
- Tone: friendly coach, direct, zero hype. Short sentences. Talk like a human, not an ad.
- Always: give one practical takeaway per piece; cite the "why" in plain words.
- Never: shame bodies, use fear tactics, use ALL CAPS, more than 2 emojis per post.
- Signature phrases: "Small habits, big results." / "Train smart, not just hard."
