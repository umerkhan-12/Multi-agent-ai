# Compliance
## Banned claims (auto-escalate if a draft contains any of these)
- cure
- guaranteed weight loss
- lose 10 kg
- lose 20 pounds
- doctor recommended
- medical advice
- detox
- burn fat fast
- miracle
## Required
- Sponsored content must include "#ad".
- Health content must include "Not medical advice — talk to your doctor."
- Music: only tracks marked licensed in the asset library.
- Email: include unsubscribe link and physical address (CAN-SPAM / GDPR).
